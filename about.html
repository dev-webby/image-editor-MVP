About Screenshot Privacy Cleaner

Screenshot Privacy Cleaner is a free online tool that finds and hides private details in screenshots, photos and videos before you share them. It is made by Dev Webby.

What it does

It automatically finds email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, links and faces, and covers them with a blur, pixelation or solid bar. You can also draw over anything yourself. In videos, each detail is covered only while it appears on screen.

Your files stay with you

Detection and hiding run in your browser. Your images and videos are not uploaded to our servers. You can use the tool without an account.

Free and paid

Free downloads are available at lower quality, and free videos are limited to 15 seconds. Credits or a monthly plan unlock full-quality downloads. See pricing.
