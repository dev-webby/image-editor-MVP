Questions

Is my image uploaded?

No. Detection and hiding run in your browser.

Do I need an account?

No. You only need one for full-quality downloads.

What can it find?

Emails, phone numbers, card numbers, links and faces. You can also draw over anything yourself.

How do I hide an email in a screenshot?

Add your screenshot, tick Emails, press Find private details, check the highlighted boxes, then download. Read the full guide.

Can I hide an email or a face in a video?

Yes. Use the Video tab. It scans the whole clip and covers each detail only while it is on screen. Read the guide.
