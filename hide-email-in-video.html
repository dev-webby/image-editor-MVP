How to hide an email in a video

Short answer: Open the Video tab, add your clip, press Scan, check the boxes it finds, then export. Each detail is covered only while it appears on screen. Free for clips up to 15 seconds.

Screen recordings often show email addresses and account details for a few seconds. Instead of marking every frame by hand, the Video tool finds them for you and hides each one only while it is on screen.

Hide an email in my video

Step by step

  1. Open the Video tool and choose your clip.
  2. Tick Emails and anything else you need, then press Find private details in the whole video.
  3. The tool checks the clip about every half second. Each detail is covered at the place and time it appears.
  4. Press Play to check, untick or draw to adjust, then press Create redacted video.

Does the cover follow the email?

The cover appears where and when each email is found. It works best when text stays in one place, such as a settings screen. If text scrolls, the cover moves in short steps and may lag a little, so check the result before you share it. Clips up to 15 seconds are free. Longer clips or full quality use credits.

Questions

Can I blur faces in the same video?

Yes. Tick Faces and they are followed automatically.

Is my video uploaded?

No. It runs in your browser. Chrome or Edge works best.
