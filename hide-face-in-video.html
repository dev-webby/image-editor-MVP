How to blur a face in a video

Short answer: Open the Video tab, add your clip, tick Faces, press Scan, check the boxes and export. The cover follows the face. Always preview the result and add manual areas for any face it missed.

Maybe you need to hide a bystander, a child, or yourself before sharing a clip. The Video tool finds faces on every frame and covers them as they move.

Blur faces in my video

Step by step

  1. Open the Video tool and choose your clip.
  2. Make sure Faces is ticked.
  3. Pick Pixelate, Blur or Black bar, then press Play to check the result.
  4. Press Create redacted video and download it.

What to check before you share

Faces are found best when they are clear and not very small. Watch the whole clip once, and draw over any face the tool misses. Pixelate or a black bar hides more than a light blur.
