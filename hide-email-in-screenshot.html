How to hide an email in a screenshot

Short answer: Add your screenshot to Screenshot Privacy Cleaner, tick Emails, press Find private details, check the highlighted boxes and download the cleaned image. It is free and runs in your browser.

Screenshots often show email addresses, phone numbers and card numbers you did not mean to share. To redact an email in a screenshot, you cover it so it cannot be read. Here is how to do it in about a minute.

Hide an email in my screenshot

Step by step

  1. Open the Privacy Cleaner and add your screenshot. You can upload it, drag it in, or paste it.
  2. Tick Emails. Tick Phone numbers or Card numbers too if they appear. Then press Find private details.
  3. Check the highlighted boxes. Untick any you want to keep, and drag over anything the tool missed.
  4. Download the cleaned image.

Blur, pixelate or black bar?

A light blur can sometimes be partly reversed. Pixelate or a black bar removes the detail from the image, so use one of those for emails and card numbers.

Questions

Does it work on photos too?

Yes, as long as the text is clear. You can always draw over an area yourself.

Is my screenshot uploaded?

No. Everything runs in your browser and the image never leaves your device.
